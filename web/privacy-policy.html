Pavitra Mandir · Android

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 October 2026
Application ID: com.pavitramandir.app

Your worship experience stays private.

Pavitra Mandir saves preferences and worship timestamps on your device to support its virtual temple experience. Daily worship does not require an account. Optional Google sign-in enables saved progress, coins, item unlocks and WhatsApp invitations. Android backup is disabled for app data. We do not include advertising trackers.

1. About this policy

This policy explains how Pavitra Mandir for Android handles information when you use the app. The app is provided by Bhuwan Chandra Joshi ("we", "us", or "our").

2. Information the app handles

The app stores the following information in its private storage on your device:

  • Worship and temple-cleaning timestamps: used to restore the temple's state and reflect time since your previous activity.
  • Language and curtain-colour preferences: used to remember your display choices.

Ritual interactions are also processed on your device to run the current worship session. Worship activity can reveal aspects of religious practice. If you sign in, completed puja dates, coin rewards, unlocks, temple selections and account history are saved to our account service. We do not use them to create advertising profiles.

Google sign-in provides your verified email address and Google account identifier. We receive a temporary identity token to verify sign-in and issue a session. For referral attribution, the app sends the Play Store referral ID, install time, and a one-way digest of an app-scoped device identity. We do not receive your Google password, access your contacts or messages, or collect precise location, camera, microphone or photo data.

3. Collection and sharing

When signed in, the app transmits the account and reward information described above to our account service. WhatsApp sharing opens a message containing an opaque invitation link. You choose the recipient and whether to send it. WhatsApp handles that message under its own privacy policy; we cannot read the message or verify delivery. We record share handoffs and qualifying new installations to maintain your rewards. We do not sell app user data or share it for targeted advertising.

Android backup is disabled for app data. Google Play and your device provider may separately process information when you download, update, back up, or use your device under their own policies, including the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Android permissions

  • Vibration: allows tactile feedback during app interactions. This permission does not access personal information.
  • Internet: is declared in the app. It connects to Google sign-in, our account service and online bhajan audio sources. Temple visuals and ritual sounds are bundled for offline worship.

5. Storage, backup, and security

Saved preferences and timestamps are held in Android's app-private storage, protected by the operating system's access controls. No method of storage is completely secure.

Android backup is disabled for this app. Account sessions are protected using Android Keystore. Signed-in account state is stored by our account service and retrieved when you sign in. Release builds use HTTPS for account requests.

6. Retention and deletion

Local preferences and timestamps remain until replaced by later activity or removed. To delete local app data, open Android Settings → Apps → Pavitra Mandir → Storage → Clear storage / Clear data, or uninstall the app. Menu names vary by device. Clearing data resets your saved preferences and temple state.

Clearing local data does not delete a server account. Use My profile → Delete account and activity to remove your account, worship history, coins, unlocked items, saved selections and invitation links. Minimal one-way digests of registered device identities and previously rewarded Google account identifiers remain to prevent repeated referral rewards after deletion or reinstallation. These records are pseudonymous and are used only for reward abuse prevention. Contact us for privacy or deletion questions.

7. Contact and privacy requests

For privacy questions or requests, contact Bhuwan Chandra Joshi at bhuwanchandra.it@gmail.com.

If you email us for support or privacy inquiries, your email message and address are processed through our email service provider (Gmail / Google Workspace) solely to answer your inquiries and provide user support. We do not use support communications for marketing or share them with third parties. Support emails are retained only as long as necessary to address your request or until you request deletion.

8. Children's privacy

The practices described above apply to all users. The app does not request a user's age. Optional Google registration collects the account information described above. A parent or guardian with a privacy concern can contact us using the details above.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or our data practices change. The updated policy will be published on this page with its effective date. Where required, we will provide additional notice and obtain consent before new data processing begins.
