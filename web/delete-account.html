Pavitra Mandir · Account & Data Deletion

Delete Account and Data

App: Pavitra Mandir (com.pavitramandir.app)
Developer: Bhuwan Chandra Joshi

Two ways to delete your account:

You can delete your account immediately inside the Pavitra Mandir Android app, or submit a web deletion request below if you no longer have the app installed.

1. Immediate deletion inside the app

If you still have Pavitra Mandir installed on your device:

  • Open Pavitra Mandir and open the profile/menu drawer.
  • Tap My profile → Delete account and activity and confirm.
  • Your server account, profile, worship history, coins, unlocked items, and sessions are removed immediately.

2. Request account deletion on the web

Use this form to request deletion of your Pavitra Mandir account and associated cloud data without reinstalling the app.

You can also email bhuwanchandra.it@gmail.com from your registered Google email address.

3. What data is deleted and what is retained

  • Permanently deleted: your account record, verified email address, saved shrine selections, unlocked items, Punya Coin balance and passbook history, completed puja dates, outbound WhatsApp share links, and active login sessions.
  • Retained for abuse prevention only: minimal one-way cryptographic digests of previously registered app-scoped device identities and previously rewarded Google account subjects are retained solely to prevent repeated referral coin rewards after account deletion or reinstallation. These digests cannot be used to restore your profile.
  • Processing timeline: verified web deletion requests are processed within 7 days. In-app deletion takes effect immediately.
  • Local device data: to remove preferences stored locally on your phone, use Android Settings → Apps → Pavitra Mandir → Storage → Clear storage or uninstall the app.
