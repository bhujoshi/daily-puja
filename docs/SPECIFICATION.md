# Nitya Mandir (नित्य मंदिर) - Product & Ritual Specification

## 1. Executive Summary & Vision

**Nitya Mandir (नित्य मंदिर)** is a sacred, culturally authentic daily virtual worship playground designed for Indian users and the global Sanatan diaspora. It translates the deeply personal, tactile, and spiritual experience of an authentic Indian home temple (घरेलू पूजा घर) into a digital sanctuary on Android and iOS.

### Core Philosophy
- **Authenticity over Gamification**: Traditional worship is mindful and reverent. The application does not trivialize devotion with arcade scoreboards; instead, it fosters authentic spiritual discipline through realistic interactions, gentle mindfulness gates, and natural consequences (such as dust settling on neglected altars and flowers withering into *nirmalya*).
- **Sensory Realism**: Digital brass clinks and resonates with true acoustic decay, the sacred shankh produces deep 432 Hz resonant vibrations, the diya flame wavers naturally in soft breeze, and flower petals fall with soft organic weight.
- **Bilingual Default (Hindi First)**: Full native support for Hindi (हिंदी - default) and English, using respectful Vedic and Puranic terminology.

## 3. The Purity Gate (शुद्धि संकल्प / Snan Confirmation)

In traditional Indian worship, one never enters the home temple without taking a bath, cleansing hands, and wearing clean attire.

### Rules & Mechanics:
1. When the user launches the app each day, the temple is concealed behind **ornate silk curtains (पट / पर्दा)** tied with golden brocade tassels.
2. An interactive bottom dialog or ribbon appears in authentic Hindi:
   > **"क्या आपने स्नान कर शुद्धि प्राप्त कर ली है?"**  
   > *(Have you bathed and purified yourself?)*
3. Two affirmative actions:
   - **"हाँ, मैं शुद्ध हूँ" (Yes, I am clean)**
   - **"स्नान मंत्र श्रवण करें" (Listen to the Snan Mantra)**: Plays *"गङ्गे च यमुने चैव गोदावरि सरस्वति..."* for mindful grounding.
4. Once affirmed, the silk curtains glide apart with a realistic fabric rustle accompanied by a subtle brass chime, illuminating the sacred murtis.

## 4. The 8-Step Daily Worship Ritual (अष्टपदी नित्य पूजा विधि)

Each step must be conducted in traditional sequence:

```
[1. Samagri Sangrah] -> [2. Deepa Prajwalan] -> [3. Dev Snan] -> [4. Tilak & Pushpa]
         |
[8. Aarti Stuti]    <- [7. Bhog Samarpan]   <- [6. Shankh Naad] <- [5. Ghanti & Aarti]
```

### Step 1: Samagri Sangrah (सामग्री एकत्रीकरण / Preparation)
- **Concept**: Gathering holy water, fresh flowers, chandan, and fresh offerings.
- **Interaction**: The user taps items to assemble their personal brass Pooja Thali:
  - Fresh Jal / Gangajal in a brass Lota.
  - Fragrant fresh Marigolds (गेंदा) and Roses (गुलाब).
  - Pure Sandalwood paste (चन्दन) and Kumkum (रोली).
  - Fresh Naivedya (Peda, Ladoo, Batasha).
- **Feedback**: Soft metallic clinks as each item settles onto the carved thali.

### Step 2: Deepa Prajwalan & Dhoop (दीप प्रज्वलन एवं धूप / Light the Diya)
- **Concept**: Dispelling darkness and inviting divine light (*तमसो मा ज्योतिर्गमय*).
- **Interaction**: User strikes a virtual matchstick or guides the sacred flame to the brass diya wick. The flame catches and immediately casts dynamic, warm golden light across the murtis. The user also lights fragrant Agarbatti, generating translucent curls of rising smoke.
- **Mantra Displayed**: *"शुभं करोति कल्याणम् आरोग्यम् धनसंपदा..."*

### Step 3: Dev Snan & Abhishek (देव स्नान एवं मार्जन / Bathing the Gods)
- **Concept**: Purifying the deities with holy water and wiping them dry.
- **Interaction**:
  - The user tilts the sacred brass lota / shankh over Ganesh Ji and Lakshmi Ji.
  - Animated water streams and glistening droplets wash gently over the idols.
  - The user takes a soft yellow/red silken cloth (वस्त्र मार्जन) and gently pats the deities dry.
- **Mantra Displayed**: *"ॐ अपवित्रः पवित्रो वा सर्वावस्थां गतोऽपि वा..."*

### Step 4: Tilak & Pushparpan (तिलक एवं पुष्पांजलि / Tilak & Flower Offering)
- **Concept**: Adorning the deities with auspicious fragrant pastes and blossoms.
- **Interaction**:
  - **Tilak**: User dabs yellow Chandan on Ganesh Ji's forehead and Lakshmi Ji's forehead, followed by auspicious red Kumkum/Akshat.
  - **Pushparpan**: User picks fresh marigolds and lotus flowers and showers them at the feet and shoulders of the deities, with soft gravity and gentle settling physics.
- **Mantra Displayed**: *"ॐ श्री गणेशाय नमः | ॐ महालक्ष्म्यै नमः"*

### Step 5: Ghanti & Aarti (घंटी वादन एवं आरती / Aarti with Brass Bell)
- **Concept**: Waking the cosmos and warding off negative energies through sacred sound and sacred flame.
- **Interaction**:
  - Left Hand / Tap: Rings the hanging brass temple bell (घंटी). Real brass bell harmonics with pitch decay and tactile haptic beats.
  - Right Hand / Drag: Rotates the flaming Aarti Thali in rhythmic, clockwise circles (प्रदक्षिणा भाव) around the murtis.
- **Feedback**: Ambient luminescence peaks; golden sparkles swirl around the sanctum.

### Step 6: Shankh Naad (शंख नाद / Blowing the Conch)
- **Concept**: Purifying the entire atmosphere with the primordial cosmic sound of creation.
- **Interaction**: User presses and holds the sacred white Shankh.
- **Feedback**: A powerful, authentic 432 Hz resonant acoustic conch call unfolds, with progressive volume, reverberating echoes, and continuous deep vibrational haptics. Concentric sonic ripples expand visually across the screen.

### Step 7: Bhog & Naivedya Samarpan (भोग एवं प्रसाद समर्पण / Offering Prasad)
- **Concept**: Dedicating the day's nourishment to the Divine before partaking.
- **Interaction**:
  - User places the silver/brass Bhog thali before the deities (Modak for Ganesh Ji, Kheer/Makhana for Lakshmi Ji).
  - The user performs the sacred *Achaman* (जल आचमन) gesture by waving the water flower three times in clockwise circles, reciting: *"ॐ प्राणाय स्वाहा, ॐ अपानाय स्वाहा, ॐ व्यानाय स्वाहा..."*

### Step 8: Aarti Stuti & Audio Recitation (आरती गायन / Play Aarti)
- **Concept**: Complete surrender in devotional song.
- **Interaction**:
  - Built-in sacred audio player offering classic aartis:
    1. *Jai Ganesh Deva (जय गणेश जय गणेश जय गणेश देवा)*
    2. *Om Jai Lakshmi Mata (ॐ जय लक्ष्मी माता)*
    3. *Sukhkarta Dukhharta (सुखकर्ता दुखहर्ता)*
  - Features real-time synchronized karaoke-style bilingual lyrics (Hindi Devanagari & English transliteration/meaning), bell sound accompany toggle, and background playback capability.
- **Resolution**: Upon completion, the temple radiates a peaceful, divine glow. A personalized blessing appears: *"पूजा संपन्न हुई। आपका दिन मंगलमय हो।"*

## 5. Temporal Aging & Temple Cleaning Engine (मंदिर शुद्धि चक्र)

In real life, a home altar naturally ages: uncleaned flowers dry and wither into *nirmalya*, the oil lamp burns out leaving quiet soot, and fine dust settles on the altar platform.

### Mathematical Aging Model
Let $\Delta t$ be the elapsed time in hours since the last completed pooja:

$$\text{Dust Level } D(\Delta t) = \min\left(1.0, \; \frac{\Delta t - 14}{72}\right) \quad (\text{for } \Delta t > 14\text{ hours})$$

$$\text{Flower Withering } F(\Delta t) = \begin{cases} 
0.0 & \Delta t \le 12\text{ hours} \text{ (Fresh)} \\
\min\left(1.0, \; \frac{\Delta t - 12}{24}\right) & \Delta t > 12\text{ hours} \text{ (Fading to Nirmalya)}
\end{cases}$$

### Visual State Transitions
| Elapsed Time | Temple Status | Visual Manifestation | Required Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0 – 12 hours** | Pristine Sanctum | Diya glowing, fresh fragrant blossoms, serene incense smoke | Day's worship complete |
| **14 – 24 hours** (Next Morning) | Needs Morning Cleaning | Diya extinguished with small soot mark, flowers turned dull/wilted (*nirmalya*), faint layer of dust | Morning Cleaning Ritual required before starting new pooja |
| **48 – 72 hours** (2-3 Days) | Neglected | Distinct grey dust layer on altar chouki, dried curled brown flowers, unwashed bhog bowl | Deep cleaning required |
| **7+ Days** | Heavy Neglect | Prominent dust particles, spider-silk traces, dull brass, solemn quiet tone | Complete temple restoration |

### Morning Cleaning Ritual (सवेरे की सफाई)
Before a new pooja can begin on subsequent days:
1. **Nirmalya Sangrah (निर्माल्य संग्रह)**: User taps and collects wilted flowers into a sacred brass *Nirmalya Patra* (विसर्जन पात्र) to be returned to nature.
2. **Chouki Pouchha (चौकी मार्जन)**: User drags a soft wet cleaning cloth across the temple base, erasing the dust texture procedurally with realistic swipe trails.
3. **Diya Shodhan (दीपक प्रक्षालन)**: The brass diya is rinsed clean, ready to receive fresh ghee and cotton wick (बाती).
