Checks: normal-version title matching; decoded 20-second sample; sample bitrate at least 112 kbps (allows VBR), sample rate at least 32 kHz, and metadata/estimated duration between 1 and 40 minutes. Excluded named sped-up, 8D, remix, ringtone, lecture and repeated-loop versions. Sample loudness metadata attenuates unusually loud files; it is not a full-track mastering pass. These are technical checks, not a full human listening review.
