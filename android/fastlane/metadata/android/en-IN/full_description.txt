🪔 COMPLETE DAILY PUJA RITUALS (नित्य पूजा विधि)
Open directly into your home sanctum and follow a guided step-by-step daily worship flow designed for all ages:
1. Diya & Dhoop (दीप एवं धूप): Light Single Ghee Diya, Panchmukhi Diya, or Mayur Deepstambh alongside Agarbatti, Loban, Royal Kamal Dhoop, or Mayur Dhoop with natural rising smoke.
2. Dev Snan & Abhishek (देव स्नान): Bathe the deity with sacred water from a traditional copper Kalash.
3. Sacred Tilak (तिलक धारण): Apply ceremonial Kumkum, Kesar-Chandan, and Akshat tilak to the deity's forehead.
4. Pushparpan (पुष्पांजलि): Adorn the shrine with Marigold (Genda), Rose, Jasmine Mogra, Lotus, Orchid, or Lily garlands and shower fresh petals.
5. Shankh Naad (शंख नाद): Blow the sacred Panchajanya or Swarna Shankh tuned to 432 Hz, resting on a sculpted brass tripod stand.
6. Bhog & Prasad (भोग समर्पण): Offer Motichoor Laddu, Sooji Halwa, Tirupati Laddu, or Kesar Modak.
7. Ghanti & Aarti (घंटी एवं आरती): Perform circular Aarti with Traditional, Golden, or Nagkanya Panch-Aarti thalis accompanied by resonant temple bells.
